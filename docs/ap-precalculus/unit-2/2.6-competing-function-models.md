## 📖 Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Asymptotic Dominance:** Exponential growth functions ($b > 1$) eventually outgrow any polynomial function of degree $n$, no matter how large the leading coefficient or degree.
- **Model Evaluation:** Examining residuals and domain constraints to determine which function model provides the most valid physical interpretation.

---

## 📐 Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Asymptotic Dominance Limit:**

$$\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{a \cdot b^x}{c \cdot x^n} = \infty \quad (b > 1, \; c > 0)$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    Even though a polynomial like $y = 1{,}000 x^{10}$ may be much larger than $y = 1.01^x$ for small values of $x$, the exponential model will ALWAYS exceed the polynomial for sufficiently large $x$.
