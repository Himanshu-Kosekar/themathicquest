## 📖 Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Arithmetic Sequences:** Additive constant common difference $d$. Successive terms change by addition: $a_n = a_{n-1} + d$. Discrete analog of linear functions.
- **Geometric Sequences:** Multiplicative constant common ratio $r$. Successive terms change by multiplication: $g_n = g_{n-1} \cdot r$. Discrete analog of exponential functions.

---

## 📐 Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Arithmetic Explicit Formula:**

$$a_n = a_1 + (n - 1)d$$

**Geometric Explicit Formula:**

$$g_n = g_1 \cdot r^{n - 1}$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    Notice the $(n - 1)$ exponent in geometric sequences when index starts at $n = 1$. When index starts at $n = 0$, the formula simplifies to $g_n = g_0 \cdot r^n$.
