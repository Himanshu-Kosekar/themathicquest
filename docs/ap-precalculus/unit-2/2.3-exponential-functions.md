## Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Standard Form:** $f(x) = a \cdot b^x$ where $a \neq 0$, $b > 0$, and $b \neq 1$.
- **Growth vs. Decay:**
- - $b > 1$: Exponential Growth ($f(x)$ increases as $x$ increases).
- - $0 < b < 1$: Exponential Decay ($f(x)$ decreases as $x$ increases).
- **Key Features:**
- - Horizontal Asymptote at $y = 0$.
- - $y$-intercept at $(0, a)$.
- - Domain: $(-\infty, \infty)$; Range: $(0, \infty)$ for $a > 0$.

---

## Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Exponential Function Form:**

$$f(x) = a \cdot b^x = a(1 + r)^x$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    The base $b$ can NEVER be negative! If $b < 0$, the expression $(-2)^x$ is not defined for fractional exponents in real numbers.
