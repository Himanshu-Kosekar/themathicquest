## Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Definition:** $(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))$. The output of $g$ becomes the direct input to $f$.
- **Domain of Composite Function:** All inputs $x$ in the domain of $g$ such that $g(x)$ is within the domain of $f$:
- $$\text{Domain}(f \circ g) = \{x \in \text{Domain}(g) \mid g(x) \in \text{Domain}(f)\}$$
- **Decomposition:** Breaking a composite expression into distinct inner and outer component functions.

---

## Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Function Composition:**

$$(f \circ g)(x) = f\big(g(x)\big)$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    To find the domain of $f(g(x))$, first restrict $x$ to the domain of inner function $g$. Then solve the inequality requiring $g(x)$ to satisfy the domain conditions of outer function $f$.
