## High-Yield AP Exam Recommendations

1. **Precision in Justifications:** Whenever asked to justify that a function is increasing or concave up, explicitly compute the **Average Rate of Change (AROC)** across consecutive intervals.
2. **Limit Notation for End Behavior:** Always express horizontal asymptotes using standard limit notation:
   $\lim_{x \to \infty} f(x) = L \quad \text{and} \quad \lim_{x \to -\infty} f(x) = L$
3. **Factoring First:** When analyzing rational functions, factor completely to immediately distinguish **removable discontinuities (holes)** from **infinite discontinuities (vertical asymptotes)**.
4. **Logarithmic Domains:** Always check for extraneous roots in candidate solutions to logarithmic equations ($x > 0$).
