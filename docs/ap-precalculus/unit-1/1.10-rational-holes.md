## Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Removable Discontinuities (Holes):** Occur at $x = c$ when $(x - c)$ is a shared factor in both numerator and denominator, with multiplicity in numerator $\ge$ multiplicity in denominator.
- **Coordinates of the Hole:** The $x$-coordinate is $c$. The $y$-coordinate is found by taking the limit of the simplified expression: $y = \lim_{x \to c} R(x)$.

---

## Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Hole Coordinates Formula:**

$$R(x) = \frac{(x - c)P_1(x)}{(x - c)Q_1(x)} \implies \text{Hole at } \left(c, \; \frac{P_1(c)}{Q_1(c)}\right)$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    On an AP exam, justify a hole by stating: (1) $f(c)$ is undefined, and (2) the two-sided limit $\lim_{x \to c} f(x) = L$ exists as a real finite number.
