## 📖 Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Linear Functions:** Characterized by a constant average rate of change over equal intervals: $\text{AROC} = m = \text{constant}$.
- **Quadratic Functions:** The average rate of change is itself a linear function of the input. Consecutive equal-interval differences of AROC are constant (constant second differences).
- **Vertex & Symmetry:** A quadratic function $f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c$ changes direction at its vertex $x = -\dfrac{b}{2a}$, where the instantaneous rate of change transitions from positive to negative (or vice versa).

---

## 📐 Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Linear Constant Rate of Change:**

$$f(x) = mx + b \implies \text{AROC}_{[x_1, x_2]} = m \quad \text{for all } x_1 \neq x_2$$

**Quadratic Vertex Location:**

$$f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c \implies x_{\text{vertex}} = -\frac{b}{2a}$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    To verify if tabular data represents a quadratic function over equally spaced inputs, check if the first differences are changing linearly (i.e., the second differences are constant).
