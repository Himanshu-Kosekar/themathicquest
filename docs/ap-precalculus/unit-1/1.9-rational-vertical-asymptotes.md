## Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Vertical Asymptotes:** Occur at $x = c$ where the denominator equals zero after all common factors with the numerator have been canceled.
- **One-Sided Limit Characterization:** The function approaches $\infty$ or $-\infty$ from each side:
- $$\lim_{x \to c^+} R(x) = \pm\infty \quad \text{and} \quad \lim_{x \to c^-} R(x) = \pm\infty$$
- **Multiplicity Rule for Asymptotes:**
- - Odd Multiplicity in Denominator: The curve goes to opposite infinities on either side of the asymptote.
- - Even Multiplicity in Denominator: The curve goes to the same infinity on both sides of the asymptote.

---

## Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Vertical Asymptote Limit Criterion:**

$$\lim_{x \to c^+} f(x) = \pm\infty \quad \text{or} \quad \lim_{x \to c^-} f(x) = \pm\infty \implies x = c \text{ is a vertical asymptote}$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    Vertical asymptotes must be written as linear equations $x = c$. Writing just the number "$c$" on an AP exam will result in loss of credit.
