## Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Geometric Modeling:** Expressing perimeter, surface area, volume, and optimization problems in terms of a single variable.
- **Cost & Revenue Modeling:** Constructing profit functions $P(x) = R(x) - C(x)$ and finding break-even points.
- **Interpreting Results:** Solving for contextual values within valid restricted domains.

---

## Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Profit Optimization Model:**

$$P(x) = R(x) - C(x) = x \cdot p(x) - C(x)$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    Draw a diagram and identify all given constraints. Use constraint equations to eliminate extra variables so your final objective function has strictly one independent variable.
