## 📖 Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Model Selection Criteria:** Matching contextual physical constraints (such as asymptotes, bounded capacities, or symmetric peaks) to function families.
- **Linear:** Constant rates of change.
- **Quadratic:** Parabolic trajectories, projectile motion, single peak or trough with symmetric growth/decay.
- **Rational:** Quantities with natural saturation ceilings, fixed budget allocations ($y = C/x$), or asymptotic limiting behavior.
- **Assumption Articulation:** Explicitly identifying domain restrictions dictated by real-world physical limits (e.g., non-negative time, dimensions, or capacities).

---

## 📐 Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Residual Calculation:**

$$\text{Residual} = y_{\text{actual}} - y_{\text{predicted}}$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    On the AP exam, when asked to "articulate assumptions," state realistic physical limitations on domain (e.g., "$t \ge 0$ because time cannot be negative") and explain why the model cannot be extrapolated indefinitely.
