## 📖 Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **General Transformation Model:** $g(x) = a \cdot f(b(x - h)) + k$.
- **Vertical Transformations (Outside the Function):**
- - $a > 1$: Vertical stretch by factor $|a|$; $0 < a < 1$: Vertical compression.
- - $a < 0$: Reflection across the $x$-axis.
- - $k$: Vertical shift (up if $k > 0$, down if $k < 0$).
- **Horizontal Transformations (Inside the Function):**
- - $b > 1$: Horizontal compression by factor $\frac{1}{|b|}$; $0 < b < 1$: Horizontal stretch.
- - $b < 0$: Reflection across the $y$-axis.
- - $h$: Horizontal shift (right if $h > 0$, left if $h < 0$).

---

## 📐 Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Canonical Transformation Equation:**

$$g(x) = a \cdot f\big(b(x - h)\big) + k$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    Always factor out the coefficient $b$ inside the function argument! In $f(2x - 6)$, rewrite as $f(2(x - 3))$ to see that the horizontal shift is right by $3$, not $6$.
