## Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Locating Zeros:** The zeros of a rational function $R(x) = \dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}$ are the values of $x$ where the numerator $P(x) = 0$ and denominator $Q(x) \neq 0$.
- **Sign Charts:** Partitioning the domain using all real zeros and vertical asymptotes as boundary test points to establish intervals where $R(x) > 0$ and $R(x) < 0$.

---

## Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Zero Condition for Rational Functions:**

$$R(x) = 0 \iff P(x) = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad Q(x) \neq 0$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    Always check candidate zeros in the denominator before declaring them as $x$-intercepts! If a candidate makes both numerator and denominator zero, it is a hole, not a real zero.
