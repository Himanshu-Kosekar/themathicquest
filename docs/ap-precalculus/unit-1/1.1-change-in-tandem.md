## 📖 Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Covariation of Quantities:** Investigating how changes in an independent variable ($x$) produce corresponding increases or decreases in a dependent variable ($y$).
- **Qualitative Analysis:** Identifying intervals where a function is increasing ($x_1 < x_2 \implies f(x_1) < f(x_2)$) or decreasing ($x_1 < x_2 \implies f(x_1) > f(x_2)$).
- **Directional Rate of Change:** Interpreting real-world scenarios such as water filling irregularly shaped vessels, tracking distance vs. time, or height vs. angle.

---

## 📐 Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Directional Change Condition:**

$$\Delta x > 0 \implies \begin{cases} \Delta y > 0 & \text{Function is Increasing} \\ \Delta y < 0 & \text{Function is Decreasing} \end{cases}$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    When asked to describe change in tandem on an AP Free-Response Question, always explicitly state the direction of both variables simultaneously: e.g., "As time $t$ increases from $0$ to $5$, the height of the fluid increases at a decreasing rate."
