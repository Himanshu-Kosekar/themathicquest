## Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Leading Term Dominance:** As $x \to \pm\infty$, the highest degree term $a_n x^n$ dominates all lower-degree terms.
- **Limit Notation:** End behavior must be articulated using formal calculus limits: $\lim_{x \to \infty} P(x)$ and $\lim_{x \to -\infty} P(x)$.
- **Even vs. Odd Degree Rules:**
- - Even Degree ($n$ even, $a_n > 0$): Both ends point up: $\lim_{x \to \pm\infty} P(x) = \infty$.
- - Even Degree ($n$ even, $a_n < 0$): Both ends point down: $\lim_{x \to \pm\infty} P(x) = -\infty$.
- - Odd Degree ($n$ odd, $a_n > 0$): Left end down, right end up: $\lim_{x \to -\infty} P(x) = -\infty$, $\lim_{x \to \infty} P(x) = \infty$.
- - Odd Degree ($n$ odd, $a_n < 0$): Left end up, right end down: $\lim_{x \to -\infty} P(x) = \infty$, $\lim_{x \to \infty} P(x) = -\infty$.

---

## Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Polynomial End Behavior Dominance:**

$$\lim_{x \to \pm\infty} P(x) = \lim_{x \to \pm\infty} a_n x^n$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    Never write arrow notation like "$x \to \infty, y \to \infty$" on the AP Exam unless explicitly instructed. Always write formal limit notation: $\lim_{x \to \infty} f(x) = \infty$.
