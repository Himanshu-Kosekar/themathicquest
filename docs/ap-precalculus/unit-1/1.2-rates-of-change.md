## Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Average Rate of Change (AROC):** Measures the net change in output per unit change in input across a closed interval $[a, b]$.
- **Geometric Meaning:** AROC equals the slope of the secant line intersecting $(a, f(a))$ and $(b, f(b))$.
- **Units of Rate of Change:** Units are always $\dfrac{\text{units of } f(x)}{\text{units of } x}$ (e.g., meters per second, degrees Celsius per hour).

---

## Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Average Rate of Change (AROC):**

$$\text{AROC}_{[a, b]} = \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{f(b) - f(a)}{b - a}$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    Always specify correct dimensional units whenever computing AROC in a contextual problem. Missing units is one of the most common causes of point deduction on AP exams!
