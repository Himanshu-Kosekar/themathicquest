## Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Fundamental Theorem of Algebra:** A polynomial of degree $n \ge 1$ has exactly $n$ complex zeros, counted with multiplicity.
- **Complex Conjugate Root Theorem:** If a polynomial with real coefficients has a non-real complex zero $a + bi$, then its conjugate $a - bi$ is also a zero.
- **Multiplicity Behavior on Graphs:**
- - Odd Multiplicity ($m = 1, 3, \dots$): The graph crosses the $x$-axis at the root.
- - Even Multiplicity ($m = 2, 4, \dots$): The graph touches (bounces off) the $x$-axis.

---

## Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Linear Factorization Theorem:**

$$P(x) = a_n (x - z_1)(x - z_2)\cdots(x - z_n) \quad (z_i \in \mathbb{C})$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    Non-real complex zeros always appear in conjugate pairs $(a \pm bi)$ for polynomials with real coefficients. If degree $n$ is odd, the polynomial must have at least one real zero!
