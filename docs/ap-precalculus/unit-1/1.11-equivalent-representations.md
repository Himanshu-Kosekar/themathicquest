## 📖 Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Rewriting Rational Expressions:** Dividing numerator by denominator to decompose improper rational functions into quotient plus remainder: $R(x) = q(x) + \dfrac{r(x)}{d(x)}$.
- **Vertex Form of Quadratics:** Completing the square to rewrite $f(x) = a(x - h)^2 + k$, revealing the vertex $(h, k)$ and axis of symmetry.
- **Factored Form:** Revealing roots, multiplicities, and axis intersections.

---

## 📐 Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Polynomial Division Algorithm:**

$$\frac{P(x)}{D(x)} = Q(x) + \frac{R(x)}{D(x)} \quad \text{where } \deg(R) < \deg(D)$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    Rewriting an improper rational function into polynomial quotient plus proper remainder immediately reveals its end behavior and slant asymptote!
