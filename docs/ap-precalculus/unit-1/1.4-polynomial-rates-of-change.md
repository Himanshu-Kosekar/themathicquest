## 📖 Core Concepts & Learning Objectives

- **Local Extrema:** Points where a polynomial transitions between increasing and decreasing behavior.
- **Concavity:** A function is concave up on intervals where its rate of change is increasing; it is concave down where its rate of change is decreasing.
- **Points of Inflection:** Locations where the concavity changes from concave up to concave down or vice versa.

---

## 📐 Essential Formulas & Definitions

**Concavity Rate of Change Criteria:**

$$\begin{aligned} \text{AROC is increasing} &\iff \text{Graph is Concave Up} \\ \text{AROC is decreasing} &\iff \text{Graph is Concave Down} \end{aligned}$$

!!! tip "AP Exam Tip"
    On AP Precalculus Free-Response Questions, never use calculus words like "second derivative" for concavity justifications. Use College Board required phrasing: "The function is concave up because the rate of change is increasing."
